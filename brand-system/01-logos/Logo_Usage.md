# Logo usage

## Approved artwork in this package

- `certiva-wordmark-primary.svg`: green lockup for white and pale backgrounds.
- `certiva-wordmark-reversed.svg`: white lockup for Night/Surface backgrounds.
- `certiva-icon.svg`: compact square mark for avatars and constrained UI.
- `certiva-favicon.svg`: 32 px favicon source.

These are verbatim copies of the website's current SVG artwork as of 5 October 2026. They are not redraws of the previously approved refined logo.

The `*-640.png` files are high-resolution derivatives of those same SVGs, rendered with the original Inter wordmark font. They are embedded in the social and document templates so a missing local font cannot change the logo. Keep the SVGs as the master artwork.

## Placement

- Keep a clear area of at least 11 px around the 44 px icon; scale the clear area proportionally.
- Minimum wordmark width: 120 px digital, 30 mm print. Below that, use the icon.
- Place the logo once per composition. Use it at top left on editorial and product layouts unless the format calls for a centered avatar.
- Use primary on White/Mist/Pale. Use reversed on Night/Surface/Forest. Do not place it on busy screenshots without a solid backing panel.
- Never stretch, rotate, outline, add shadows, recolor the artwork, detach the check symbol, or fuse a tagline into the lockup.

## Wordmark production warning

The website SVG contains editable `<text>` with an Inter font reference rather than outlined vector letters. For handoff to print vendors, provide a flattened high-resolution export or outline the type from a verified Inter installation first. Do not allow a fallback font to silently alter the wordmark. The source font and license are included in `fonts/` only to reproduce the existing logo; Certiva's new supporting typography is Manrope.

## Future refined-mark replacement

The previously approved `certiva-mark-refined.svg` is currently unavailable because its iCloud copy is not downloaded. When recovered, compare it with the production icon, obtain explicit approval for the new master, then update all derivatives and templates together. Do not substitute an approximation.
