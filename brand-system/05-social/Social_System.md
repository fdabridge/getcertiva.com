# Social production system

Certiva posts should look like clear product and operations reporting, not generic software hype. An asset should teach one operational point even if the viewer never clicks through.

## Content families

1. **Workflow proof:** application approval, audit set, signing, review, certificate handoff - one task per post.
2. **Operator insight:** a handoff that creates rework and a practical way to control it.
3. **Product detail:** a verified feature shown in a real UI crop.
4. **Evaluation clarity:** implementation, migration, security or support questions answered honestly.
5. **Founder perspective:** why the operating problem matters, without manufacturing scale or authority.

## Cover formula

- One short eyebrow: `WORKFLOW / 01`, `PRODUCT DETAIL`, or `OPERATOR NOTE`.
- One specific headline, ideally under 9 words.
- One proof frame or one explanatory diagram; do not combine several unrelated modules.
- One Certiva logo, with protected clear space.
- Optional footer: `getcertiva.com` and a useful action, not a second logo.

## Files and dimensions

- `LinkedIn_Post_1200x627.svg`: LinkedIn landscape / link cover.
- `Instagram_Post_1080x1350.svg`: portrait feed post.
- `Instagram_Square_1080x1080.svg`: square feed / carousel card.
- `Instagram_Story_1080x1920.svg`: story and highlight source.

The SVGs are editable working templates. Their supporting copy uses bundled Manrope; the logo is an embedded raster derivative of the unchanged production SVG and should be replaced only from the logo master. Sample copy is illustrative, not an approved claim about every implementation. When a template contains a product placeholder, replace it with a current, permission-cleared screenshot before publishing. Export at native dimensions; do not stretch a square asset into portrait.

## Safe zones

- Feed: at least 5% outer margin; keep important text out of bottom 10% on Instagram.
- Story: keep critical content inside x=72..1008 and y=220..1640 px. Leave top and bottom interface zones clear. Do not rely on a tiny website URL as the only call to action; use a link sticker when available.
- LinkedIn banner, if adapted separately: protect the left profile-picture overlap and verify desktop/mobile crops.

## Captions

Use a plain opening, a short description of the workflow, the human control point, and one clear next action. Do not put every feature into one caption. Do not make “AI” the subject unless the post accurately demonstrates an implemented AI-assist function and its review boundary.
