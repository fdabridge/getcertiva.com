# Color specification

The current website palette is a quiet green system. Use dark surfaces to frame evidence and product imagery, not to turn every asset into a glowing AI advertisement.

| Role | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Night | `#060D08` | Primary dark canvas |
| Surface | `#0D1710` | Dark panels and product frames |
| Border | `#1A2E1E` | Dividers on dark |
| Forest | `#1A4731` | Primary brand mark, light-background headlines and buttons |
| Mid | `#2D6A4F` | Secondary green, light-background labels |
| Leaf | `#40916C` | Large accents on light; readable text on Night |
| Signal | `#52C27A` | Highlights and action signals on Night only |
| Pale | `#D8F3DC` | Soft green card surfaces, dark-background light text |
| Mist | `#F0FAF4` | Quiet light panels |
| Ink | `#111827` | Primary body text on light |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Light canvas and dark-background text |

## Pairings and accessibility

- Ink on White: 17.74:1. Forest on White: 10.56:1. Mid on White: 6.39:1.
- Signal on Night: 8.75:1. Pale on Night: 16.63:1. White on Forest: 10.56:1.
- Do **not** use Signal on White for paragraph text (2.24:1) or Leaf on White for small text (3.83:1). Leaf can serve large display type at 3:1+ but Forest is safer.
- Error, warning and success states must use text/icon plus color, not color alone.
- Keep live product screenshots color-accurate; do not recolor the actual interface for promotion.

## Proportions

For a typical marketing asset: 65–80% Night or White, 15–25% Surface/Mist, and no more than 5–10% green highlight. Do not use gradients behind body copy. The signal color is a pointer, not a flood fill.
