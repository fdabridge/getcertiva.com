# Typography

Use **Manrope** for Certiva marketing, documents and interface work. Its geometric detail makes the brand more distinctive without making operational content feel experimental. The font files and license are bundled in `fonts/`, and the website loads the variable TTF locally. Use Arial/Helvetica only as a technical fallback. Use a real monospace family sparingly for product labels or structured records; never make the brand look like a terminal demo by default.

Headlines are specific and left-aligned. Prefer sentence case. Avoid all-caps paragraphs and excessive green words. A social cover should have no more than three headline lines and one primary emphasis treatment.

For documents, use 10.5–11 pt body copy, 14–20 pt headings, and at least 1.35 line spacing. Tables need clear labels, visible rules and enough room for long organization names.

For scripts not covered by Manrope, use the appropriate Noto Sans script family. Do not letter-space Arabic or other connected scripts. Check native speakers for external multilingual copy.
