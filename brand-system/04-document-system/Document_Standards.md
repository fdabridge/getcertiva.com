# Document standards

The files here are editable layout starters, not legal or commercial terms. They do not identify the contracting entity, scope, price, security commitment or governing law. Complete those fields in each actual proposal.

## Proposal / product brief

- Name the recipient and the exact operational question being addressed.
- Show one workflow and the desired controlled outcome before listing platform modules.
- State what is in scope now, what requires configuration, and what is not available.
- Include migration, access/security, support and pilot assumptions when relevant.
- State the legal seller and authorized signatory separately from the Certiva product brand.
- Include date, version, author, confidentiality marking when needed and a specific next step.

## Letterhead

- Use the green primary wordmark on white. Keep at least 20 mm outer margin on A4.
- Use Ink for body text, Forest for key headings, subtle gray-green dividers.
- Keep a quiet footer with website and an editable legal-entity field. Do not hard-code an entity that has not been verified for the document.

## Controls

- Never insert a personal signature, certification stamp or accreditation seal into a reusable template.
- Never let an illustrative product diagram be mistaken for a real customer record.
- Export final external PDFs with text selectable and links working; visually inspect every page.
